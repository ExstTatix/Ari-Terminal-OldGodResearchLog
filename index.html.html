OPENING LOG #1553 OF DR. A. CARTER, AKA DEPUTY HEAD OF ASTRONOMICAL RESEARCH

Joint studies between the Apollyon Research Unit and the Astronomical Research Committee have uncovered what can only be described as a non-localized anomaly:
an entity beyond known space, referred to only as one of the “Old Gods.” That’s not a title we gave it. That’s the name it’s used. Repeatedly.

We know it exists because it’s sending us something, radio waves. Not from a planet, a satellite, or a star.
These transmissions appear seemingly out of nowhere, somewhere between the midpoint of our known universe and its outer boundary.
There’s no origin point, no trajectory, just signals that exist without warning. And what’s worse is that they travel faster than light.
According to everything we understand about physics, that should be impossible.

Each transmission references the last. Not directly, but there’s a pattern. It’s like it's trying to tell a story in reverse, or maybe in circles.
Some messages echo fragments of biblical scripture, though often distorted, reworded, or placed in strange contexts that feel deliberately unsettling.
We’ve only seen one other thing like this. . . NULLK.

That anomaly behaved similarly. At one point, we even believed it might be “God.”
But whenever we questioned it about the other entity, the one it referred to as one of the OLD GODS in all caps, it would go silent.
Not just unresponsive, but completely static. Pure static. Intentional. Like fear, but without emotion. It's the only reported time it's done this too.

To even detect these new signals, we had to create something called the Negative Mass Container (NMC).
It's a device capable of isolating negative mass particles, the kind that vanish when they come into contact with regular or massless matter.
The technology behind it goes beyond what people get taught in physics classes. Somehow, the NMC allows us to hold these impossible signals in place and translate them into sound.

What we hear doesn’t make sense at first. The words are fragmented, cryptic, and strange.
But with time, the transmissions begin to connect. The patterns repeat, references emerge, and eventually it starts to feel like it’s answering us.
It knows we’re listening.

We don’t know what it is. Or what it wants.
Maybe it doesn’t want anything.
Maybe it’s just watching.
Or maybe it’s waiting.
We’ll keep listening. But between you and me?
I’m not sure we should.

Listen, I don't know if this message will be seen by the right people, but I need your help.
I’m convinced the Foundation’s Overseers are hiding the truth from us.
We’ve been told that the Administrator’s power comes from the Old Gods — more accurately, the Primordial gods — but there’s more to the story than we’re being told.
The Nullk revealed information about a fallen, malevolent deity — an ancient entity that once held dominion over our universe.
According to the Nullk, this being is directly tied to the lineage of the Primordials.
While searching the Administrator’s office, I found a hidden document tucked away in one of his desk drawers. I’ve encrypted it with a code.
Your mission is to decipher that code and enter it on the secure website linked below.
